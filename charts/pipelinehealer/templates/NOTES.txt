1. PipelineHealer has been deployed.

2. Get services:
   kubectl get svc -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

3. If ingress is disabled, port-forward frontend:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pipelinehealer.frontend.fullname" . }} 3000:{{ .Values.frontend.service.port }}

4. Backend health check:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pipelinehealer.backend.fullname" . }} 8000:{{ .Values.backend.service.port }}
   curl http://127.0.0.1:8000/health
